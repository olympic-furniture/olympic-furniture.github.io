# Design System Scope

## Overview

This repository has two distinct visual surfaces. The established homepage at `/` retains its existing light blue, red and pale yellow identity and recognizable business logo. Its source of visual truth remains the shared components and [stylesheet](src/styles.css).

The separate modern wood preview at `/wood-preview/` has a scoped [design system](src/wood-preview/DESIGN.md) and [machine-readable sidecar](src/wood-preview/.impeccable/design.json). Its warm paper, walnut and olive tokens apply only to that alternate route. They do not replace the homepage identity.

No global replacement palette is established by this index. Read the route's source and scoped design document before extending a surface.
